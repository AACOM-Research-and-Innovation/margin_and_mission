# Card impact differentials and full scoring guide

This guide describes the shipped 14-card simulation. It is generated from the investment catalog and event resolvers, followed by the scoring formulas used by the app. Raw mission, strategic and resilience points are converted into category scores; they are not final score points.

## How to interpret a differential

Each result card compares the actual event outcome with a scenario that removes one held card and keeps all other investments. This isolates the card’s conditional effect on that event. It does not refund its startup cost, remove its recurring expense from the annual operating result, or recompute the entire prior simulation. Those costs and direct raw points are reported separately. Conditional benefits overlap: do not add all card comparisons together.

For the storm, maintenance (card 1) reduces the event cost from $3M to $1.5M. Continuity (card 2) changes the mission penalty from −5 to −1 raw points. Both can operate independently. For ransomware, controls (card 3) reduce cost from $2.4M to $300K; adding insurance (card 4) reduces the controlled incident cost to $50K. Removing controls also invalidates insurance, so those two comparisons overlap.

Annual operations are calculated before the year’s events. The Financing Act enrollment reduction therefore changes the operating revenue of later years. A recurring expense reduction from the estate gift begins after Year 3 operations and affects subsequent projections. The full estate gift adds $2.5M to operating reserves and reduces annual rural-track expense by $250K; it does not refill the starting restricted gift.

## Full scoring formulas

Let `clamp(x, a, b) = min(b, max(a, x))`. Current days cash is `reserve / ((baseline expenses + annual expense changes) / 365)`. Raw points accumulate from purchases and events.

### Financial health — maximum 35

```text
10 × clamp(reserve / 20,000,000, 0, 1)
+ 5 × clamp(unspent investment fund / 5,000,000, 0, 1)
+ 10 × clamp(current days cash / 120, 0, 1)
+ 10 × clamp((cumulative operating result + 3,000,000) / 6,000,000, 0, 1)
```

Subtract 10 if the lowest recorded days cash falls below 60. Set financial health to zero if the lowest recorded days cash is below 45 or citations reach two. Clamp the result to 0–35. The historical low begins at 98 days and is updated after each year’s events. Event cash impacts change reserves; they do not change the cumulative operating result. Startup spending changes the investment fund and restricted gift, not the annual operating result.

### Mission and outcomes — maximum 30

```text
12 × clamp((enrollment index − 0.85) / 0.20, 0, 1)
+ 18 × clamp(raw mission points / 40, 0, 1)
```

### Strategic position — maximum 20

`20 × clamp(raw strategic points / 35, 0, 1)`

### Resilience — maximum 15

`15 × clamp(raw resilience points / 24, 0, 1)`

### Total and facilitator adjustments

Add the four category scores and all facilitator point rulings, then clamp to 0–120. The four categories sum to at most 100; positive adjustments can take the total above 100, up to 120. Category limits mean an additional raw point can have no effect when a category is already saturated. Tables rank by total score.

The Year 1 preceptor Board Ask is a mentor ruling rather than an automatic event. Use facilitator adjustments to apply its outcome. Cash adjustments change reserves; point adjustments change total score. They remain recorded on the final report.

## Recorded scores, projections and rollback

The projection screen shows category changes against the preceding recorded year (the opening position is the Year 1 baseline). Green arrows indicate increases; red arrows indicate decreases; a flat arrow indicates a change that rounds to zero. The line graph displays total score from Year 0 through completed years. After Year 3, dashed lines add five projected years, Years 4–8. Projections hold purchased investments, enrollment, expenses and rulings fixed, honor delayed revenue, and add annual operating results to reserves and cumulative results. No new events, purchases or rulings are assumed. Historical liquidity breaches and citations continue to affect scores. These are scenarios rather than predictions.

The CFO financial outlook is a separate rolling five-year financial view. It shows annual operating results and reserve balances and can be expanded during deliberation, after submissions, and alongside results.

The facilitator stage order is deliberation → close → show events → calculate → show results → next year (or publish final standings after Year 3). Rollback moves back one stage. Rolling back from calculated results to events restores the ledger before that year while preserving submitted cards, decider names, dissent and facilitator rulings. CFOs can explicitly edit a retained submission when deliberation reopens.

# Financial display and timing

The participant interface separates the one-time investment fund, annual operating result, purpose-restricted gift and cash reserve. Startup uses the restricted gift first for eligible choices, including a partial restricted balance. The selection panel and top display use the same preview; purchase resolution uses the same restricted-first allocation.

The three-year view holds current enrollment fixed and includes existing investments and the selected draft. It excludes future events, board rulings and future purchases. Closed years use the recorded operating result, while current and future years show the known plan. The reserve is a recorded cash balance, not a forecast. A compact summary remains visible while browsing choices. Short red flags identify prerequisite card numbers; green flags identify delayed annual revenue; an orange flag marks rural-track restricted-gift eligibility. Funding-source text is omitted from the other cards.

All 15 choices, seven events and three rounds remain enabled. Revenue starts in the purchase year plus the configured lag. The interface uses the actual start year and identifies revenue outside the exercise. Annual costs begin on purchase and continue. Points and event protection retain their current immediate ownership behavior; no new maturation rule was introduced. Institutional research provides its additional signal in subsequent briefs. Actual revenue-start years replace the generic lag label on revenue cards. The blanket points-and-protection sentence has been removed from non-revenue cards. The unused lag fields on OMM succession and rural training do not currently delay their points or event protection; this is a model decision to resolve separately, not a rule introduced by the display revision.

## Session briefing

Explain that the investment fund is allocated once for the entire exercise and unspent money carries forward. Annual revenues and expenses recur; an operating surplus is not a second investment-fund allocation. Reserves absorb operating results and event cash effects. Use rural-track startup to demonstrate why an eligible restricted gift does not eliminate annual operating expense. Use the AI program with governance to demonstrate startup spending, annual expense from Year 1 and revenue beginning in Year 2.

## Verification and limits

Focused tests compare the restricted-funding preview with purchases for full, partial and exhausted gifts; check delayed program revenue and the enrollment-sensitive bridge; ensure prior purchases are not charged twice; and check that the three-year preview does not reveal events. Existing scenario and rendering checks cover three rounds and reference portfolio outcomes.

The event engine and scoring weights are unchanged. Immediate event protection, long-horizon investment incentives, scoring explanations and structured board rulings remain supervisor discussion topics. The richer results explanation proposed separately has not been implemented. This is a participant-display revision, not a change to the educational model.
